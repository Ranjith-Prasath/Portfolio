01 About

I'm a Master's student in Data Science at UCLouvain, with a background in Computer Science and a growing interest in applied AI, data systems, automation and sustainable computing.

Most of my hands-on experience comes from building RevenU, a financial analytics and reconciliation platform using Python, PostgreSQL, Flask, Docker and data-processing pipelines. Although the application is built around financial data, the project gave me practical exposure to backend workflows, databases, APIs, reconciliation algorithms, testing and data-intensive systems.

Within RevenU, I have also experimented with Generative AI components including RAG, ChromaDB-based retrieval, LLM-assisted parsing and basic LLM-driven workflows. I am now building on that foundation while learning more about AI engineering, LLM systems and infrastructure.

My Master's thesis focuses on the environmental impacts of different Generative AI user profiles. I am particularly interested in understanding how AI usage translates into computational workloads, energy demand, water consumption and broader environmental impacts.

02 Research

Master's Thesis — Environmental Impact of Generative AI

Evaluation of the environmental impacts of different Generative AI user profiles.

  • Investigating how different patterns of GenAI usage translate into computational workloads and environmental impacts.
  • Studying indicators including greenhouse-gas impact, primary energy demand, water use and resource consumption.
  • Exploring the connections between LLM inference, workload characteristics, computing infrastructure and environmental efficiency.
  • Examining how different GenAI user behaviours — from occasional conversational use to more intensive workflows — may change environmental impact at scale.
